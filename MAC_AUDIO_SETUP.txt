HOW TO START THE APP ON MAC

1. Make sure macOS has a Spanish voice:
   System Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak
   Set System speech language to Spanish and choose/download a Spanish voice.

2. Double-click START_APP.command.

That's it.

START_APP.command will:
- detect your installed Spanish Mac voice,
- automatically generate any missing listening audio,
- verify that the files exist,
- start a local web server,
- open the app in your browser.

IMPORTANT:
Do not open index.html directly for the listening exercises.
Always use START_APP.command.

To switch to a different Spanish Mac voice:
1. Change the voice in System Settings.
2. Double-click REGENERATE_SPANISH_AUDIO.command.
3. Double-click START_APP.command again.

Your config.js / Groq setup is unchanged.
